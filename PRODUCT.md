# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Small business owners in Israel (typically 1–20 person businesses: restaurants, clinics, contractors, service providers) who feel invisible online. They are not technically savvy, do not know SEO or AI-visibility vocabulary, and have tried "having a website" without seeing results. They arrive suspicious of digital agencies and need to feel respected, not sold to.

## Product Purpose

JOJO Digital offers a free, no-commitment digital visibility check — an audit of how visible a business is on Google, AI tools (ChatGPT/Gemini/Perplexity), Google Business Profile, and social platforms. The landing page's single conversion goal is to get the visitor to fill in their name, phone, business name, and optional website. JOJO contacts them within 48 hours with real findings.

## Brand Personality

Sharp, honest, local. No agency fluff. The brand positions itself as the opposite of a generic digital agency: direct, specific, and results-oriented. Three words: ישיר / מקצועי / אמיתי (direct, professional, real).

## Anti-references

- Generic Israeli digital agencies with vague service language ("נגיע לתוצאות" / "נוביל אותך להצלחה")
- SaaS landing pages with hero metrics and identical feature card grids
- Dark-mode pages overloaded with neon/cyan glows and gradient text
- Any layout where the logo is the dominant visual element

## Design Principles

1. **Specificity over superlatives.** Name what is checked, how it works, and what the visitor gets. No "world-class" or "cutting-edge."
2. **Respect the reader.** The target audience is a business owner who has been burned by vague promises. Every line should say something concrete.
3. **Conversion without pressure.** The CTA is free and no-commitment — the copy should reinforce that without repeating it three times in the same section.
4. **Dark premium, not dark SaaS.** The dark navy background and amber accent should feel professional and trustworthy, not tech-startup generic.
5. **One accent, everywhere.** Amber is the only accent color. No secondary palette creep.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

RTL Hebrew throughout. WCAG AA minimum contrast. Reduced-motion support via existing CSS and framer-motion. Touch-friendly tap targets (≥44×44px).
